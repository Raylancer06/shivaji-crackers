Nationwide Freight Clarity

Transport Logistics & Parcel Delivery FAQ

Safe, legal, and verified dispatch methods direct from Sivakasi regional transport warehouses.

01

How are crackers dispatched from Sivakasi to other cities?

Pyrotechnics are classified as hazardous freight and cannot be shipped through domestic air courier (such as BlueDart, DTDC, or India Post). All shipments leave Sivakasi strictly through government-approved heavy parcel lorries (such as VRL, ARC, SRS, Kranti, or MSS Transport) to your local city hub. You will receive an official Lorry Receipt (LR) tracking note.

local_shipping Licensed Heavy Carriers Consignment LR Provided
02

What is the minimum order quantity for parcel dispatch?

To ensure safe industrial wooden-frame corrugated packing and freight viability from Sivakasi, our minimum order quantity (MOQ) for interstate transport dispatch is ₹3,000 total invoice value. This protects products from transit pressure and ensures cost-effective freight rates for buyers.

inventory_2 Corrugated Reinforcement Standard MOQ: ₹3,000
03

Can I visit the Sivakasi factory showroom directly?

Yes, our retail showroom and factory display facility in Paraipatti on Sivakasi-Sattur Main Road is open 7 days a week from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM. During the peak Diwali month (September - November), our customer counters remain open 24 hours daily with parking space for wholesale vans and visiting families.

storefront Walk-in Shoppers Welcome Open 7 Days a Week
04

How does payment and invoice confirmation work?

Once your estimate is finalized, our coordinator shares an itemized tax invoice. Payment is executed via secure direct NEFT/RTGS bank transfers or UPI directly into our official factory account. Cash on Delivery (COD) is strictly prohibited by freight carriers for firework consignments.
