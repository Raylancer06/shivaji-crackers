## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a high-trust, festive-yet-contemporary digital shopping and wholesale quotation experience for Sivakasi fireworks commerce. It moves decisively away from cluttered, chaotic catalog templates into an elevated, structured aesthetic that celebrates celebration itself.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Festive Luxury:** Combines the visceral vitality of traditional celebrations—fiery ambers, rich royal crimson, and illuminated golds—with deep slate structures and immaculate whitespace.
- **Glassmorphic Precision:** Subtle blurred glass overlays for navigation bars, sticky price estimate trays, quick-view sheets, and promotional ribbons ensure visual richness without compromising utility or load speed.
- **Institutional Reliability:** Pyrotechnic purchases demand safety verification, regulatory adherence, and bulk logistical transparency. Clean layouts, clear badge architectures, and prominent legal compliance callouts reassure both direct consumer households and wholesale bulk buyers.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** Retail families preparing for Diwali, weddings, and cultural festivals, as well as pan-India wholesale distributors and event planners demanding fast estimate generation, verifiable licensing, and tier discounts.
- **Emotional Response:** Euphoric, safe, premium, transparent, and effortlessly organized.

## Colors

The color palette shifts the regional firecracker e-commerce paradigm from harsh oversaturated backgrounds to an editorial, intentional hierarchy. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#8B1E1E` - Royal Crimson):** Anchors brand authority, primary call-to-actions, category header panels, and header accents. Reflects traditional Sivakasi craft heritage and royal celebration.
- **Secondary (`#FF7A00` - Vibrant Marigold):** Spark, energy, and festival illumination. Applied to badges, offer alerts, price-drop ribbons, estimate triggers, and high-impact hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#1E293B` - Midnight Slate):** Grounding neutral tone for wholesale toolbars, top utility bars, heavy data tables, and high-contrast typography.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Ink):** Ultra-dark slate for all primary readable body copy, tabular numbers, and sharp structural iconography.

### Functional & Surface Application
- Backdrops rely on `#FAFAF8` (Warm Canvas) and pure `#FFFFFF` for product card isolation, preventing color clashing with colorful firework packaging.
- Subtle gold tints (`#FFF7ED`) form container surfaces for festive bundle highlights and statutory legal advisory banners.
- Green accents (`#16A34A` / `#059669`) are dedicated strictly to green cracker certification, 100% legal compliance tags, and in-stock badges.

## Typography

Typography balances high-impact retail merchandising with geometric legibility for extensive product lists, quantities, and price calculators.

### Type Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Primary Typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans across all headings, labels, prices, and copy. Its generous counters and modern geometric architecture maintain pristine legibility on small phone screens during quick order additions.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Currency values and quantity counters utilize bold to extra-bold weight variants with strict tabular numeral spacing (`tnum`) to ensure aligned price estimation columns.
- **Legal & Compliance Callouts:** Styled cleanly at `body-sm` and `label-md` with controlled uppercase tracking to maintain maximum scan speed without visual noise.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined, 12-column responsive grid structures all product displays, bulk price tables, and filter sidebars.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px outer margins, 16px gutters. Product listings display in high-density 2-column cards or 1-column bulk rows with integrated +/- stepper counters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, 24px margins, 20px gutters. Collapsible floating estimate summaries allow unobstructed category browsing.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (1280px wide maximum content constraint) with 32px canvas margins. 3 columns dedicated to sticky filter/category navigators and live order-estimate calculators, with 9 columns for product grids (3 cards wide).

### Rhythmic Rules
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for badge interiors, stepper button padding, and status tags.
- Use `space-md` for product card internal padding and form field vertical gaps.
- Use `space-xl` for spacing between distinct category aisles (e.g., Sparklers, Multi-Sky Shots, Chakkarams, Gift Boxes).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses lightweight glassmorphic planes paired with warm-tinted ambient shadows, deliberately avoiding heavy, dark drop-shadows that clash with festive aesthetics.

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FAFAF8` flat background.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Panels):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` and a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(139, 30, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Product Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward lift (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by an enhanced shadow: `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Bars & Modals):** Sticky top-nav and bottom mobile "View Estimate / Cart" bars use glassmorphic frosted finishes: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

A modern, soft-cornered geometry (Level 2) brings approachable premium quality while preserving structured order and screen real estate for high-density catalog browsing.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Quantity Steppers):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius. Provides tactile touch targets while feeling crisp and engineered.
- **Cards, Panels, and Table Wrappers:** `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`) creates friendly, self-contained product tiles that frame vivid firework package shots.
- **Promotional Banners & Modal Dialogs:** `1.5rem` (24px) radius (`rounded-xl`) delivers a gentle, modern silhouette.
- **Pill Accents:** Pill radii (`9999px`) are reserved exclusively for contextual status chips ("Green Cracker Verified", "Flat 70% Off Sivakasi Price", "Bestseller").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Instant Estimate / Add to Inquiry):** Royal Crimson background (`#8B1E1E`), pure white text, bold font weight, `0.5rem` radius. Interactive state transitions to `#6A1414` with subtle inward press feedback.
- **Secondary Action (Download Pricelist PDF):** Midnight Slate outline or ghost treatment with amber accents (`#FF7A00`) on hover.
- **Fast Quantity Stepper:** Dual square/rounded icon buttons flanking a centered bold input, enabling rapid multi-item count additions without page shifts.

### Product & Wholesale Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white bounding container, `1rem` radius, crisp border. Includes high-resolution preview thumbnail, brand manufacturer tag (e.g., Standard, Sony, Sivaji Prime), bilingual / Tamil phonetic title, strike-through original MRP, and red highlight offer price.
- **Badges:** Absolute positioned top-left for discount percentage (`#B91C1C`) and top-right for Green Cracker certified badge (`#059669`).
- **Wholesale Table View:** Alternating rows (`#FAFAF8` / `#FFFFFF`) with tabular numeric price cells, minimum order quantity (MOQ) indicators, and quick total price calculators.

### Statutory & Legal Compliance Notice
- **Banner Architecture:** A dedicated top ribbon and footer advisory panel framed with subtle warm border, featuring Supreme Court / PESO compliance disclosures in authoritative Midnight Slate (`#1E293B`) text over a subtle warm cream background (`#FFF7ED`). Clear icons indicate safe transport, no open courier shipping, and licensed Sivakasi dispatch.

### Input Fields & Search Filter
- **Inputs:** 48px height, `0.5rem` radius, light gray border with focus rings in warm amber (`#FF7A00`) and soft ambient glow.
- **Category Filter Chips:** Horizontal scrolling pill tags with active state in solid crimson and inactive state in soft slate-surface with subtle neutral borders.

### Floating Sticky Quick-Estimate Bar
- **Mobile & Desktop Tray:** Positioned at screen bottom with a frosted glass backdrop, displaying total selected items, cumulative box count, calculated savings tally, and an instant primary button to submit for Sivakasi direct-dispatch quotation.
